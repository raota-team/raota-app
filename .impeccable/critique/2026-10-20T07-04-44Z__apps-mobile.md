## Design Specificity Verdict
Authored for RAOTA where it counts most: shop "또 갈래요" ring, taste fingerprint, ticket-machine AI curator, ramen vocabulary, soup colors only where they mean something. Discovery screens (home, map, lounge) still borrow a generic Pinterest skeleton; map pins and list rows carry no taste signal.

## What's Working
- Ramen-literate copy and marks (또 갈래요, 그릇, 단골집, 식권, 국물 색) that no other food app could reuse.
- Record form: validation scrolls to and announces the first missing field, leave guard blocks swipes, save failure keeps input and becomes "다시 시도".
- Reduce Motion honored in all 13 animating files; real second dark palette via DynamicColorIOS; swipe-back works from anywhere including scroller first pages.
